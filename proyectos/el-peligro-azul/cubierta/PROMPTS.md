# El peligro azul — prompts para la ilustración de cubierta (Límite Abisal)

Recuadro de ilustración de Límite Abisal: proporción ≈ 0,69 (vertical, 2:3). Para imprenta, mínimo
≈ 1.125 × 1.650 px (300 ppp). Sin texto dentro de la imagen: título, autor y colección los compone
`scripts/cubierta.py`.

Válidos para Higgsfield (`gpt_image_2_6`, 2:3, resolución 2k) o para pegar en ChatGPT.

## Propuesta A — La cumbre del Colombier

Vertical illustration for a 1911 cosmic-horror novel cover, absolutely no text, no letters, no signature,
no border. A narrow snowy mountain ridge at dusk in the French Jura, with a tall slender wooden summit cross.
Three lines of human footprints climb the snow toward the ridge and stop abruptly in the middle of the snow,
leading nowhere; a broken walking cane lies in the snow. Above, the sky occupies most of the image and looks
like the underside of an ocean surface seen from deep below: faint rippling light, caustic patterns, as if
the mountain were at the bottom of a sea of air. High above, barely visible, a vast translucent dark shape,
like the hull of an invisible submarine, hovers. Muted palette of deep teal, blue-green and pale ice blue,
dark charcoal edges (#1a1e1f), nocturnal pastel and fine engraving texture, quiet, ominous, sublime,
vintage book illustration style.
